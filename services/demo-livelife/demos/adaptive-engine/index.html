Module 3: Misconception identification

Each finding is a hypothesis linked to evidence. Thin evidence triggers a probe, not a label.

Module 4 and 5: Interventions and temporary groups

Groups describe a need, and last only until the re-test. The teacher can move students or change the order.

Who owns each fix

Decided by the share of the class showing the misconception.

Module 6: Differentiated instruction plan

One teacher, one group at a time. Other groups work on supervised tasks with an answer key.
